Privacy Policy

GTA 6 Guidebook · Last updated: October 28, 2026

GTA 6 Guidebook is an unofficial, fan-made app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

This policy explains what information the GTA 6 Guidebook app ("the app", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep it simple: the app has no accounts and does not send your notes or photos anywhere.

Information stored only on your device

The following stays on your phone and is never uploaded to us or anyone else:

  • Map pins, their titles, notes and categories
  • Photos you add to pins
  • Mission progress and your mission tips
  • Your language choice and Premium status

Deleting the app removes all of this data from your device.

Camera and photo library

The app asks for camera or photo library access only when you tap Camera or Gallery to add a photo to a map pin. The photo is copied into the app's own storage on your device. We do not upload, share or scan your photos, and the app does not access your photo library in the background.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system and app version
  • How you interact with ads (views and taps)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. On iOS, the app asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not agree, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's settings. To learn how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

If you buy Premium, the payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation of whether Premium is active, so ads can be turned off.

What we don't do

  • We don't have user accounts and don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't run our own analytics or tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
